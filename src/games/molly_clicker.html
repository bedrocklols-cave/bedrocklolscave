About

Molly Clicker is just a simple clicker game made in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. It is inspired by Orteil & Opti's Cookie Clicker (not to be confused with my OC, Cookie).

I made this in a day and it is a small little project to sharpen my programming skills.

Anyway, if you feel your hands hurting, stop playing and take a rest!
