About Molly

Molly Absolutely is a 27 year old bubblegum pink haired woman, with blue eyes, grey sweaters with midnight blue sleeves, deep ocean shorts, black rectangular glasses (sometimes), and black and white sneakers with grey socks. She's a quiet and reserved person who doesn't trust anyone that easily. Every action she does are careful and cautious. When around trusted people, she's more clingy and gentle.

Story

She suddenly appeared on a floating island one day. The island only has a single soil block, some rubble, and sticks, that's absolutely it. Surely there's more than meets the eye, right? Piecing everything together, she started crafting a tool and starts to break the soil block. After breaking it, a wood log appeared in its place!

However, that's not all. Because, she would eventually encounter threats from the block. After a few minutes of gathering stuff, a hostile sheep spawned and almost knocked Molly into the void. Her survival instincts kicked in and killed the sheep with the tool. Exhausted, she collected the wool and meat from the sheep, to cook it and make a bed to sleep for the next day. With the materials she gathered, she started building a house made from wood and stone, which took her 11 days to build.

During the 7th day of constructing the house, she found a treasure chest that contained 3 pieces of bread, a tree sapling, and a diamond with magical properties. The diamond might come in handy, so she securely stashed it away. She also found carrots and potatoes in her 2nd treasure chest, and proceeded to grow a garden, which finally gave her a stable source of food. After all of that, she continued to break the block, encountering even more dangerous threats like slimes, gigantic spiders, and skeletons?

28 days later, growing increasingly tired because of isolation, a broken portal suddenly appeared the next day. Hopeful for escape, she built 3 long wood planks by combining them, and using it as a bridge to cross. Even though the bridge looked sketchy, and almost losing balance, she managed to do it. There's a sign with written words, telling her to channel a beam of light with a gemstone with magical properties, inside the portal frame. She goes back to the main island, and grabbed the diamond from the chest, and goes back. Then, after following the instructions, the portal opened, releasing a blinding light. Molly hesitated for a second before going into the portal.

Then she wakes up, presumably from a nightmare, but still on her floating island home. To make sure, she took a peek at the window, and there it is; actual ground! Excited, she immediately runs outside, but accidentally tripped, and fell to the ground really hard. Three stranger girls are just passing by, they heard a faint scream and a loud thud afterwards. And then, they see Molly, in a bad state! Horrified, they immediately sent her to a nearby hospital, with one of the girls carrying her. The other girl took care of her and wished her a speedy recovery. Eventually when she recovered, they all become friends, but she became besties with the silver haired girl because they are both relatable in some way.

Those 3 girls were: Cookie, Elizabeth, and Phoebe.
