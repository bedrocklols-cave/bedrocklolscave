About Abigail

Abigail Tsunami is a 31 year old brown haired woman. She wears a black blouse and a mini skirt with gold accents, has red eyes, blue goggles with pink lenses, and leather boots. She also has a cloak, but only wears it during extensive adventures. People describe her as that badass aunt who fears nothing. But, on the inside, she's a total hothead. She really doesn't like teaching, especially incompetent ones. But deep down, she can be considerate and forgiving.

Story

She doesn't have a permanent home, but you can mostly see her on the Adventurer's Guild teaching 3 incompetent students, and taking adventure requests. Everybody knows her in the guild and beyond as the Fearless Lady, because she tackles difficult quests without breaking a sweat, at least that's what the rumors say. In reality, she gets totally beat up every time she completes a hard quest, due to impatient decisions, which leads to mistakes. All thanks to her hot-headed nature.

Actually, she is a pretty private person, that's why they never knew her life outside adventuring. But, there actually might be clues, even one before she became an adventurer!

The first clue was when a particular crew of pirates were finally arrested, Abigail saw the news on a bulletin board and was shocked and yelled; "Me crew?!". Everyone around were confused, some questioning whether she's a former pirate? She tried to deny it by saying that she's just doing a pirate impression. They eventually shrugged it off, but few were skeptical.

The second one is that she writes good books. Most people know her books about adventuring and combat, but nobody knows that a certain author by the name of "A." who is a mysterious novel author is actually Abigail herself. She made stories about pirate life, war, and much more.

With the incident from almost getting exposed as a pirate, and the initials of the author being the letter A, there was no doubt that it's Abigail.

After a week of countless speculation, she admitted to being a former pirate. But, instead of arresting her, they simply did not because she's not affiliated with them anymore. In fact, this makes her even more badass! Abigail was relieved.

She would continue on with her usual work, doing difficult tasks, and teaching.

Then one day while outside, she saw 2 adventurers with insane talent, and approached both of them. They introduce themselves as Cookie and Elizabeth, one's a young talented adventurer that can tame slimes and command them, and the other is an extremely strong girl with a deceiving body that could be mistaken for a weak human.

She offered the two to be mentored, and they both agreed! Now that she finally have competent students, she's even more motivated than ever.

Eventually, they all passed the mentorship, Abigail had to retire from teaching because it doesn't suit her, and she wants to focus more on adventuring. She would go on to focus on exploring different places, admiring the different sceneries, taking requests along the way, and making some new friends. She sometimes visits her former students, to see how they're doing.
