About Phoebe

Phoebe is a 25 year old silver haired woman, who has purple eyes, two hair strands defying gravity, cyan-green spaghetti strap top, black pleated mini skirt, pink purse, and black flat shoes with white thigh-high socks. Indoors, she only wears black bikini. She is a shy person, but also a closeted degenerate. She's not very good at communicating, even afraid to ask the simplest questions. And always tries to suppress her horny tendencies, but fails anyways.
