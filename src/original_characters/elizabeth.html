About Elizabeth

Elizabeth is a 23 year old dark blue short haired woman, with green eyes that has a star-shaped iris on the right (it was blue, but I figured that having another blue-eyed girl is too much.), a watermelon headband, a blue and black overalls dress, and dark blue boots. She's a carefree and confident person, who even dares to challenge danger. A great communicator, fighter, and has incredible physical strength, even though she appears weak.

Story

She owns a big plot of land where she grows and sells different kinds of berries such as; strawberries, blueberries, and cranberries, all by herself. But, how does she manage to cultivate so much while remaining solo? Well, she was born with immense strength and stamina, which would let her work for long periods of time. At first, she used this power to chop trees for her father, who was a lumberjack. Then she used it for self-defense to fight off thieves, and more.

Way before she became a farmer, she used to be an adventurer, with her best friend Cookie. Together, they went around completing requests, encountering dangerous threats, wandering to beautiful or dangerous places, and fending off even more thieves.

One day, a fellow expert adventurer by the name of Abigail, saw the two and they were amazed by their abilities. She approached the two and invited them to her mentorship program, and the two agreed.

From there, Elizabeth developed even more combat techniques, started using a sword as the weapon of choice, and discipline. She even managed to save Cookie from death by effortlessly killing the bear. Elizabeth is basically the vanguard of the party.

After Cookie quit from adventuring, Elizabeth followed suit after a year adventuring without her. From there, she started pursuing farming. She observed lots of farmers in the city, and it seems that they mostly grow wheat, corn, and potatoes.

And then, she got an idea to grow a very niche group of crops such as berries. So she decided to buy a plot of farming land, farming tools, blueberry seeds, strawberry seeds, and cranberry seeds. After getting all the necessities, she researched a lot of stuff about farming. And eventually started planting them.

The waiting game begins. While the crops are not ready yet, she goes to visit Cookie, just to greet her. Elizabeth was shocked to see that Cookie is now famous, and also has a business, about selling baked goods! They talked for a while, and Elizabeth mentioned about starting a berry farm. Cookie was excited to see it, mainly because she's actually in need of strawberries and blueberries, so she can bake even more desserts.

Then weeks pass, and the berries are finally ready. Once they're finally harvested, she took a handful of the berries and ate them. Her favorite is the strawberry, the least being the cranberry. Now, what is she going to do with these berries? When she saw Cookie's successful business, maybe she should start turning her farm into a business too? And so, she did just that.

She started selling them, with the first customer being her best friend, Cookie.

Cookie, now being a regular at Elizabeth's farming business, started releasing the new desserts as promised, which are Strawberry Shortcake, and Blueberry Cheesecake. And then, demand for berries skyrocket, so Elizabeth started working even harder. And it eventually becomes successful.

But, all of this success attracted jealous farmers. So jealous, that they started sabotaging her humble business, hoping to make her falter. Little did they know, she knew this was going to happen. While they were conducting their little sabotage, Elizabeth appeared behind them, and started kicking their ass. Embarrased, the rival farmers ran away with significant injuries, all thanks to her fighting roots and incredible strength.

However, they did not stop. This would go on until the present, where they had enough, and surrendered. Elizabeth forgave them, but reported them to the police and sent them to the jail anyways, because they were still committing crimes after all.
