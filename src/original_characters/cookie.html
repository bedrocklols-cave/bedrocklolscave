About Cookie

Cookie is a 20 year old blue haired girl, with gold eyes, wears a maid dress, always braided hair, and has a pet slime called Blob. She's a caring and cheerful person, who wastes no time helping someone in need. And apparently is a fan favorite all over the city, due to her likable personality. But, also an enigma, because of her ability to tame these annoying slimes, and has a successful bakery business too. What an extraordinary girl...

Story

She owns an establishment that lets you adopt slimes and buy baked goods, called Slime's Bakery & Adoption Center. However, before all of this, she used to be an adventurer alongside her best friend Elizabeth.

Back then, they would go help people fullfil their requests all around the country. Rewards may vary from small to extremely big depending on the difficulty.

But, on one fateful day, Cookie was almost killed by a giant bear, and then Elizabeth managed to save her from death.

Traumatized from that ordeal, she eventually quit adventuring, and goes back to her hometown.

Upon arrival, there was a big slime infestation on her city! She immediately asked where did these slimes come from, but the folk had no clue. Cookie had to take matters into her own hands now.

With her past experience as an adventurer, she bought an establishment with money from her old career, and tries to solve the slime problem by taming them. From there, the slimes were lured, and then started taming them all.

After 3 months, the slime problem has been solved! Cookie becomes the city's hero. However, taking care of hundreds of slimes everyday is too much for her wallet. And so, she decided to open an adoption center.

Since she's popular in the city, many people were waiting for her to open the adoption center in the entrance. And after it opened, they immediately fall in line and adopted a slime. Countless paperwork were signed and slimes were given to folks. Fortunately, since the slimes were tamed, they behaved relatively well to other people, and only need water to live.

However, there's still more slimes left, and maintaining them still burns a hole in her wallet. Fortunately, she also has a hobby as a baker, and therefore she decided to sell baked goods in the adoption center. Eventually, her business became really successful.

One day, she was bored as heck. As usual, she checked her phone, and went to open a messaging app.

But then, a friend request from a person named Phoebe Holmes appeared. Apparently, they seem to live not far away. And so, they started talking. Cookie initiated the conversation, and then talked for a good while. As weeks pass, they started talking about their struggles, to blow off some steam.

Then, Cookie realized that Phoebe is not at the best time of her life, just from analyzing her struggles. Out of nowhere, she wants to finally meet her in person. She visited Phoebe's house first, which was incredibly messy. But she shrugged it off and asked Phoebe if she could clean it for her. Phoebe has been wanting to get her life together again, and so she agreed. They cleaned together and had some fun. Then, Phoebe visited Cookie's place. She was shocked that the girl she's been talking to, is actually really famous and successful. Before they go home, Cookie gave Phoebe a gift, to make her day even better.
